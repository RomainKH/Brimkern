Stockfish 19 (lite network, single-threaded), compiled to WebAssembly by stockfish.js.
Opponent of the "brimkern vs an AI" matches on /chess. Served unmodified, as a separate program
that brimkern talks to over the UCI protocol (postMessage to a Web Worker).

Files: stockfish-19-lite-single.js, stockfish-19-lite-single.wasm
